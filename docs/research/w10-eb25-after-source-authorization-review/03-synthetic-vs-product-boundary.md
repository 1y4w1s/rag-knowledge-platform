```text

  = body produced (or owner-authorized as equivalent) on the product
    generation path under a declared capture mode
```

### Product path (A / future C)

**May claim:**

- Stream phase is reachable; After slots map from `state["content"]`/`citations`.  
- Harness forbids injecting E-B6 synthetic bodies as After content.  
- Informal observation / smoke with honest `llm_called`.

**Must not claim:**

- Formal observation completed.  
- Product LLM faithfulness from harness green alone.  
- BP-A `BOUND` against unrebounded E-B12B.  
- Silent upgrade harness → formal denom.

### Synthetic / compat (B)

**May claim:**

- Rebound gold content-string codec matches After digest.  
- Binding gate returns `BOUND` under BP-A for **compat** pairs.  
- Three-hash verification helpers work.  
- AG-1 cleared **for BP-A rebound path** (not live unrebounded path).

**Must not claim:**

- Product generation occurred (`llm_called` remains false; source is author-owned).  
- Formal T2 unsupported_rate / T3 grounded_rate.  
- Clearance of B2′ or AG-5 live rebound.  
- Use as Narrow Formal After body.
